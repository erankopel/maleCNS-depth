## Plain reading (not a new hypothesis; to be followed up as exploratory work)

- H1 (i) and H3 (2) fail for one reason: at weight >= 5 the chain mixes much more slowly than at
  weight >= 1. The gap is 0.0035 instead of 0.055, and the certified tau(P^128) is still at least
  0.67.
- The sensitivity rows locate that slow mode mainly inside the cord, not at the neck. With every
  neck-crossing class removed, the cord-side SCC alone has abs(lambda_2) = 0.9984, slower than the
  whole chain (0.9965), while the brain has 0.963.
- The neck predictions themselves, (ii) and (iii), held.
- H2 and H5 confirm that the two-neuron photoreceptor modes of the signed map are a normalisation
  effect that does not depend on the sign convention. With a floor at the median input, the
  spectrum is led by extended ellipsoid-body ring-neuron modes.
- H4: with 12 to 15 percent of each neuron's synapses below confidence 0.7, the first-order
  (Lipschitz) certificate of Sec. 4.4 is lost after 4 hops. This is a statement about that
  certificate under that class. A tighter certificate might certify further; that is an
  exploratory question, not a rescue of H4.
